# Aegis Cyber Logo Project

vamos criar uma logomarca para uma empresa de investigação digital Vanguard OSINT. Aqui está o prompt Act as an elite Brand Designer specializing in Cyber Intelligence, Security, and OSINT (Open Source Intelligence). Create a ultra-high-quality, professional, modern logo design for a digital investigation firm.

### BRAND DETAILS:

- Brand Name: AEGIS CYBER

- Tagline: DIGITAL INTELLIGENCE & OSINT

- Tone & Persona: Elite, High-Tech, Discreet, Authoritative, Premium, Cutting-Edge.

### VISUAL CONCEPT & SYMBOLISM:

- Core Emblem: Combine a sleek geometric shield (symbolizing protection and legality) with abstract network nodes/data points and a subtle stylized cyber-eye/lens in the center (symbolizing deep investigation, OSINT, and clarity).

- Aesthetics: Minimalist yet powerful. Sharp geometric lines, glowing circuit network accents, and high-tech elegance. Avoid traditional clipart, clichéd magnifying glasses, or cartoonish detective hats.

### COLOR PALETTE:

- Background: Solid Dark Slate / Obsidian Navy (#0B0F19 or #0F172A).

- Primary Accent: Glowing Cyan Blue (#38BDF8) and Emerald Mint (#10B981).

- Secondary Accent: Deep Indigo Blue (#1E1B4B).

### TYPOGRAPHY & LAYOUT:

- Font Style: Clean, modern, futuristic sans-serif typeface with wide letter-spacing (kerning).

- Alignment: Centered layout with the symbol/emblem prominently placed above the brand name "AEGIS CYBER", and the tagline "DIGITAL INTELLIGENCE & OSINT" in smaller tracking text directly below.

### RENDER QUALITY & STYLE:

- Flat vector logo design with subtle neon gradient glow on dark background, 8k resolution, crisp vector edges, professional brand presentation, Behance vector showcase style, centered, high contrast, clean presentation. --no photorealistic shadows, complex textures, or messy backgrounds.
